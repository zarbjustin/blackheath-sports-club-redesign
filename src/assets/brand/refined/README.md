# Proposed Logo Refinement Assets

These files are an optional, evolutionary refinement of the existing Blackheath
Sports Club crest. They are not connected to the live website and must not
replace the approved production crest without stakeholder approval.

## Assets

- `bsc-crest-refined.svg` and `bsc-crest-refined-2048.png`
  - Primary proposed crest for large digital and print placements.
- `bsc-mark-compact.svg`, `bsc-mark-compact-1024.png`,
  `bsc-mark-compact-64.png` and `bsc-mark-compact-32.png`
  - Simplified mark for navigation, avatars and small digital placements.
- `bsc-crest-monochrome.svg` and `bsc-crest-monochrome-2048.png`
  - Single-colour crest for restricted printing, stamps and embroidery tests.
- `bsc-lockup-horizontal.svg` and `bsc-lockup-horizontal-1800.png`
  - Horizontal format for narrow headers, partner pages and sponsorship use.

## Design Intent

- Preserve the circular crest, original heather artwork and established colours.
- Increase the heather's visual weight so the symbol reads at smaller sizes.
- Reduce the coloured ring weights for a calmer, more legible edge.
- Provide purpose-built formats instead of shrinking the full crest everywhere.

## Important

The SVG files are the editable masters. The PNG files are convenience exports.
Before formal adoption, the pack should receive club approval and be tested for
signage, embroidery, print, social avatars and accessibility at small sizes.

Regenerate the SVG masters with:

```bash
node scripts/generate-logo-refinement-assets.mjs
```
